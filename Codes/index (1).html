Key Historical Eras (300 BCE - 1947 CE)

Mauryan Empire (322-185 BCE)

Chandragupta's conquests and Ashoka's edicts on dharma shaped early India.

Gupta Empire (320-550 CE)

The Golden Age: Advances in mathematics, literature, and temple architecture.

Mughal Empire (1526-1857 CE)

From Babur to Aurangzeb: Fusion of arts, forts, and the iconic Taj Mahal.

British Raj & Independence (1858-1947 CE)

Colonial rule, freedom struggle, and partition—birth of modern India.
