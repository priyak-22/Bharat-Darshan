Gurjara-Pratihara Dynasty (8th – 11th Century)

Tangible Heritage

  • Temple architecture in North India
  • Sculptural art of Khajuraho region
  • Stone carvings and inscriptions

Intangible Heritage

  • Hindu religious traditions
  • Sanskrit learning
  • Feudal administrative system
Rashtrakuta Dynasty (8th – 10th Century)

Tangible Heritage

  • Kailasa Temple, Ellora
  • Rock-cut cave architecture
  • Temple sculptures

Intangible Heritage

  • Patronage of Jainism and Hinduism
  • Sanskrit and Kannada literature
  • Royal court traditions
Chola Dynasty (9th – 13th Century)

Tangible Heritage

  • Brihadeeswara Temple
  • Bronze sculptures
  • Advanced temple architecture

Intangible Heritage

  • Bhakti movement
  • Tamil literature
  • Maritime trade culture
Rajput Dynasties (8th – 12th Century)

Tangible Heritage

  • Forts and palaces
  • Khajuraho temples (Chandela)
  • Stone architecture

Intangible Heritage

  • Code of honour and bravery
  • Heroic ballads
  • Rajput traditions
Delhi Sultanate (1206 – 1526)

Tangible Heritage

  • Qutub Minar
  • Indo-Islamic architecture
  • Mosques and tombs

Intangible Heritage

  • Sufi traditions
  • Persian influence
  • Administrative reforms
Vijayanagar Empire (1336 – 1646)

Tangible Heritage

  • Hampi monuments
  • Temple complexes
  • Stone chariot and mandapas

Intangible Heritage

  • Bhakti traditions
  • Sanskrit and Telugu literature
  • Festivals and rituals
Bahmani Sultanate (1347 – 1527)

Tangible Heritage

  • Gol Gumbaz architecture influence
  • Fortifications

Intangible Heritage

  • Persian cultural influence
  • Deccan Islamic traditions
Mewar Kingdom (8th – 16th Century)

Tangible Heritage

  • Chittorgarh Fort
  • Rajput palaces

Intangible Heritage

  • Rajput valor traditions
  • Folk songs and legends
Kakatiya & Hoysala Dynasties (11th – 14th Century)

Tangible Heritage

  • Warangal Fort
  • Hoysaleswara Temple
  • Soapstone architecture

Intangible Heritage

  • Temple-centered culture
  • Regional languages and art
End of Medieval Period (17th – 18th Century)

Tangible Heritage

  • Forts, palaces, and regional architecture
  • Urban centers

Intangible Heritage

  • Composite culture
  • Regional traditions
  • Transition to modern India
