Battles & Events in Indian History

Discover the key battles, invasions, and turning points that shaped India’s destiny.

Battle of Hydaspes (326 BCE)

Region: Punjab, near River Jhelum

Between: Alexander the Great vs. King Porus

Outcome: Greek victory but Porus retained his kingdom under Alexander’s rule.

Battle of Kalinga (261 BCE)

Region: Kalinga (modern Odisha)

Ruler: Emperor Ashoka (Maurya Empire)

Outcome: Massive casualties led Ashoka to embrace Buddhism and non-violence.

First Battle of Panipat (1526 CE)

Region: Haryana

Between: Babur vs. Ibrahim Lodi

Outcome: Babur’s victory established the Mughal Empire in India.
