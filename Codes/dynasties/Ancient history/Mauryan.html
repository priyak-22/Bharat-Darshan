Maurya Empire (c. 321–185 BCE)

Region: Northern, Central & parts of Southern India; Modern Pakistan and Afghanistan

Capital

Pataliputra (modern Patna)

Notable Rulers

Chandragupta Maurya, Bindusara, Ashoka

Religion

Hinduism, Buddhism (especially under Emperor Ashoka)

Administration

Highly organized administration with provinces governed by officials and an efficient spy system; strong centralized taxation and justice system.

Achievements

  • Built extensive roads, forts, and irrigation systems
  • Expanded trade and diplomatic relations with Hellenistic kingdoms
  • Ashoka promoted Buddhism, moral governance, and issued the famous Edicts of Ashoka
  • Unified a vast region of the Indian subcontinent under efficient governance
