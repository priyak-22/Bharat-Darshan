Nanda Dynasty (c. 345–321 BCE)

Region: Magadha (Bihar), Northern India

Capital

Pataliputra (modern Patna)

Notable Rulers

Mahapadma Nanda

Religion

Hinduism

Administration

Strong centralized administration with a powerful army and well-managed treasury.

Achievements

  • Built a wealthy and powerful empire
  • Maintained one of the largest standing armies in ancient India
  • Laid the foundation for the Maurya Empire
