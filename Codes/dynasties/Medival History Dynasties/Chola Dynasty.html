Chola Dynasty (c. 9th–13th century CE)

Region: Southern India, centered around the Kaveri Delta (Tamil Nadu)

Capital

Thanjavur and later Gangaikonda Cholapuram (Tamil Nadu)

Notable Rulers

Vijayalaya Chola, Rajaraja Chola I, Rajendra Chola I, Kulottunga Chola I

Religion

Hinduism (especially Shaivism), with tolerance for Buddhism and Jainism

Administration

The Cholas had a highly organized administrative system divided into provinces called *Mandalam*. Local self-government through village assemblies (*Sabhas*) was a key feature. They maintained a strong navy for overseas expansion and trade.

Achievements

  • Expanded their empire across South India, Sri Lanka, and parts of Southeast Asia.
  • Built architectural marvels like the Brihadeeswarar Temple at Thanjavur.
  • Developed an efficient naval force and promoted maritime trade.
  • Encouraged Tamil literature, art, and bronze sculpture.
