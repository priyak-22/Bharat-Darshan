Vakataka Dynasty (c. 250–500 CE)

Region: Central and Southern India (Vidarbha, Deccan)

Capital

Pravarapura (modern Paunar in Maharashtra)

Notable Rulers

  • Pravarasena I
  • Rudrasena II
  • Pravarasena II

Religion

Hinduism (mainly Shaivism and Vaishnavism)

Administration

The Vakatakas followed a monarchical system influenced by the Gupta model. They maintained provincial governors and had matrimonial alliances with the Guptas.

Achievements

  • Patrons of art, culture, and Sanskrit learning
  • Contributed to the flourishing of Ajanta cave paintings during their rule
  • Maintained political and cultural harmony in the Deccan
