Dynasty-wise cultural heritage from 300 BCE to 1947 CE

Maratha Empire (1674 CE – 1818 CE)

Tangible Heritage

  • Forts like Raigad, Pratapgad, Sinhagad
  • Administrative records and seals
  • Temple restorations

Intangible Heritage

  • Swarajya ideology of Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj
  • Military administration and revenue system
  • Marathi literature and bhakti tradition
Late Mughal Period (1707 CE – 1857 CE)

Tangible Heritage

  • Red Fort, Jama Masjid, miniature paintings
  • Persian manuscripts
  • Mughal gardens

Intangible Heritage

  • Persian-influenced court culture
  • Urdu literature and poetry
  • Music traditions like Khayal
British Rule (1757 CE – 1947 CE)

Tangible Heritage

  • Colonial architecture: Victoria Memorial, Gateway of India
  • Railways, courts, colleges
  • Printed books, newspapers

Intangible Heritage

  • Modern education system
  • Indian freedom movement
  • Social reform movements
Indian National Movement (1857 CE – 1947 CE)

Tangible Heritage

  • Historical documents and resolutions
  • Photographs and memorials
  • National symbols

Intangible Heritage

  • Ideals of nationalism and unity
  • Non-violence and civil disobedience
  • Constitutional values
