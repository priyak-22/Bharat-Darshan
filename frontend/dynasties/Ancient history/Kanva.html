Kanva Dynasty (c. 73–28 BCE)

Region: Northern and Central India | Capital: Pataliputra

Capital

Pataliputra (modern Patna)

Notable Rulers

Vasudeva Kanva, Bhumimitra, Narayana, Susarman

Religion

Primarily Hinduism; continued tolerance toward Buddhism and Jainism.

Administration

The Kanvas succeeded the Shungas and ruled as Brahmin kings. They maintained a similar administrative structure to their predecessors, emphasizing regional control through local governors and strong military presence in central India.

Achievements

  • Preserved political stability in Magadha after the Shunga decline.
  • Encouraged continuation of Vedic and Sanskrit traditions.
  • Maintained trade and cultural links within northern India.
  • Kept Pataliputra as a center of learning and administration.
