Maitraka Dynasty (c. 475 – 776 CE)

Region: Western India (Gujarat) | Capital: Valabhi

Capital

Valabhi (in present-day Gujarat)

Notable Rulers

  • Bhatarka (Founder)
  • Dharasena I
  • Shiladitya I (Dharmaditya)
  • Shiladitya II
  • Shiladitya IV

Religion

Predominantly Hinduism and Buddhism; the Maitrakas were known for their tolerance toward multiple faiths.

Administration

The Maitrakas maintained a monarchical system with local governors. They continued many Gupta administrative traditions and issued copper-plate grants for land and religious donations.

Achievements

  • Valabhi became a major center of learning, second only to Nalanda
  • Supported trade and culture in western India
  • Issued numerous inscriptions and land grants, showing a stable administration
  • Maintained strong diplomatic and cultural ties with other Indian kingdoms
