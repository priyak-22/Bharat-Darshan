Rashtrakuta Dynasty (c. 8th–10th century CE)

Region: Deccan Plateau, centered around Manyakheta (modern Karnataka)

Capital

Manyakheta (modern Malkhed, Karnataka)

Notable Rulers

Dantidurga, Govinda III, Amoghavarsha I

Religion

Primarily Hinduism and Jainism, with tolerance toward other faiths

Administration

The Rashtrakutas had a well-organized administration with provinces called *Rashtras* and districts called *Vishayas*. They maintained a strong army and encouraged regional governance.

Achievements

  • Expanded their rule across large parts of South and Central India.
  • Amoghavarsha I authored *Kavirajamarga*, a classic Kannada literary work.
  • Commissioned the Kailasa Temple at Ellora, a masterpiece of rock-cut architecture.
  • Promoted trade and cultural exchange across the Indian Ocean.
