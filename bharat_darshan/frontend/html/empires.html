Southern Kingdoms (Chera, Pandya, Pallava, Chalukya, Chola)

Gurjara–Pratihara Dynasty

8th–11th century CE | Kannauj

Resisted Arab invasions and ruled much of North India.

Rashtrakuta Dynasty

8th–10th century CE | Deccan Region

Built the grand Kailasa Temple at Ellora.

Chola Dynasty

9th–13th century CE | Tamil Nadu

Rajaraja I built the Brihadeeswara Temple; known for trade and navy.

Chandela & Chauhan Dynasties

9th–12th century CE | Central & North India

Famous for Khajuraho temples and Prithviraj Chauhan’s reign.

Rajput Kingdoms

8th–15th century CE | Northern, Western & Central India

Known for their valor, architecture, and resistance to invasions; included dynasties like Chauhans, Solankis, and Paramaras.

Delhi Sultanate

1206–1526 CE | Delhi

Five dynasties—Slave, Khilji, Tughlaq, Sayyid, and Lodi—ruled North India.

Vijayanagara Empire

1336–1646 CE | South India

Founded by Harihara and Bukka; major cultural revival.

Bahmani Kingdom

1347–1527 CE | Deccan Region

First major Muslim kingdom in the Deccan; later split into Sultanates.

Mewar (Sisodia Rajputs)

13th–15th century CE | Rajasthan

Symbol of Rajput pride; rulers like Rana Kumbha strengthened its glory.

Kakatiya & Hoysala Dynasties

12th–14th century CE | Telangana & Karnataka

Promoted art, architecture, and trade.

End of Medieval Period

1498 CE | Calicut (Kerala)

Vasco da Gama’s arrival marked the beginning of European influence.

Social & Religious Reform Movements

19th century

Raja Ram Mohan Roy, Ishwar Chandra Vidyasagar, Dayanand Saraswati, Syed Ahmad Khan, etc.

Revolt of 1857

1857 CE

First major armed resistance against British rule; suppressed but marked a turning point.

Formation of Indian National Congress

1885 CE

Founded by A.O. Hume to provide a platform for political dialogue.

Partition of Bengal & Swadeshi Movement

1905–1911 CE

Anti-partition protests; rise of nationalist fervor.

Home Rule Movement

1916–1918 CE

Led by Bal Gangadhar Tilak and Annie Besant for self-governance.

Jallianwala Bagh Massacre

1919 CE

British troops opened fire on peaceful protesters at Amritsar.

Non-Cooperation Movement

1920–1922 CE

Led by Gandhi; boycott of British goods, institutions.

Civil Disobedience Movement

1930–1934 CE

Salt March, refusal to obey unjust laws.

Quit India Movement

1942 CE

Mass protest demanding an end to British rule during WWII.

Independence of India

15 August 1947 CE

British rule ended; India and Pakistan became independent nations.
