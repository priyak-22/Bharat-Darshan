Gauda Empire (c. 6th – 7th century CE)

Region: Bengal (present-day West Bengal and Bangladesh)

Capital

Karnasuvarna (near Murshidabad, West Bengal)

Notable Rulers

  • Shashanka (founder and most prominent ruler)

Religion

Hinduism (Shaivism was dominant)

Administration

The Gauda Empire maintained a centralized monarchy with regional governors. Shashanka was known for strengthening local administration and military organization in Bengal.

Achievements

  • Unified large parts of Bengal under a strong native rule
  • Encouraged temple construction and promoted Shaivism
  • Played a major political role in early medieval northern India
  • Established Gauda as a significant regional power
