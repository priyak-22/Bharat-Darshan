Cultural Heritage of India

Explore India’s timeless art, architecture, traditions, and monuments that reflect its rich cultural legacy.

Ancient Indian Heritage

c. 3000 BCE – 600 CE

Rise of Harappan cities, Mauryan pillars, Gupta temples, stupas, rock-cut caves at Ajanta and Ellora, and classical Sanskrit literature.

Medieval Indian Heritage

c. 600 CE – 1700 CE

Forts, mosques, palaces, Indo-Islamic architecture, Bhakti and Sufi traditions, temple towns, and cultural fusion.

Modern Indian Heritage

c. 1700 CE – 1947 CE

Museums, colonial architecture, revival of arts, national movements, and preservation of historical monuments.
