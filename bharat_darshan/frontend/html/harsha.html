Harsha Kingdom (c. 606 – 647 CE)

Region: Northern India (Kannauj, Thanesar, parts of Bengal and Orissa)

Capital

Thanesar and later Kannauj

Notable Ruler

Harshavardhana (Harsha)

Religion

Initially a Shaivite Hindu, later became a devoted Buddhist and supported religious tolerance.

Administration

Harsha maintained a well-organized administrative system similar to the Guptas. His empire was divided into provinces governed by officials. He emphasized welfare, justice, and diplomacy, and maintained diplomatic relations with China under Emperor Taizong of Tang.

Achievements

  • Reunified much of northern India after the fall of the Gupta Empire
  • Promoted literature, art, and religious tolerance
  • Authored plays like *Nagananda*, *Ratnavali*, and *Priyadarsika*
  • Held grand Buddhist assemblies at Kannauj
  • Encouraged learning at Nalanda University
