Gurjara-Pratihara Dynasty (c. 650 – 1036 CE)

Region: Northern and Western India (Rajasthan, Gujarat, and parts of Uttar Pradesh)

Capital

Kannauj

Notable Rulers

  • Nagabhata I
  • Vatsaraja
  • Nagabhata II
  • Mihira Bhoja (Bhoja I)
  • Mahendrapala I

Religion

Hinduism (especially Vaishnavism), with tolerance toward Buddhism and Jainism

Administration

The Gurjara-Pratiharas had a strong feudal structure. The empire was divided into provinces governed by feudatories. The king maintained control through loyalty and tribute. Kannauj became a major political and cultural center under their rule.

Achievements

  • Defended northern India against Arab invasions from Sindh
  • Revived the glory of Kannauj and stabilized northern India
  • Encouraged temple building and Sanskrit learning
  • Flourishing of art and sculpture under royal patronage
  • Played a central role in the Tripartite Struggle with the Palas and Rashtrakutas
