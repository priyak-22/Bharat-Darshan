Shunga Dynasty (c. 185–73 BCE)

Region: Northern and Central India | Capital: Pataliputra

Capital

Pataliputra (modern Patna)

Notable Rulers

Pushyamitra Shunga, Agnimitra

Religion

Predominantly Hinduism; Buddhism continued to flourish under some rulers.

Administration

The empire was governed by kings supported by ministers and regional governors. The Shungas maintained a strong military and focused on restoring Brahmanical traditions after the Mauryan decline.

Achievements

  • Revival of Hindu art, culture, and Sanskrit literature.
  • Promotion of education through institutions like Takshashila and Nalanda.
  • Expanded political control in parts of northern and central India.
  • Contributed to the development of early Indian sculpture and architecture (notably the Bharhut Stupa).
