Satavahana Kingdom (c. 1st century BCE – 3rd century CE)

Region: Deccan (Andhra Pradesh, Maharashtra, parts of Madhya Pradesh)

Capital

Pratishthana (modern Paithan, Maharashtra)

Notable Rulers

  • Simuka
  • Gautamiputra Satakarni
  • Vashishtiputra Pulumavi
  • Yajna Sri Satakarni

Religion

Hinduism and Buddhism

Administration

The Satavahanas ruled through a centralized monarchy with provincial governors. They used Prakrit in inscriptions and promoted trade and agriculture.

Achievements

  • Revived Vedic culture and promoted Brahmanism
  • Supported Buddhist architecture — Sanchi, Amaravati, and Nasik caves
  • Issued silver and lead coins with inscriptions in Prakrit
  • Maintained strong trade relations with the Romans via ports on the west coast
