Region: Magadha (modern Bihar), Northern India

Capital

Pataliputra (modern Patna)

Notable Rulers

  • Mahapadma Nanda (Founder)
  • Dhana Nanda (last ruler, defeated by Chandragupta Maurya)

Religion

Primarily Hinduism

Administration

The Nandas established a strong centralized state with immense wealth and a powerful army. They maintained a complex taxation system and extensive bureaucracy inherited from the Magadhan tradition.

Achievements

  • Expanded Magadha’s territory through conquest and diplomacy
  • Maintained one of the largest standing armies in ancient India
  • Strengthened economic prosperity through efficient administration
  • Laid the foundation for the Mauryan Empire
