Vijayanagar Empire (1336–1646 CE)

Region: Southern India, mainly Karnataka and Andhra Pradesh

Capital

Vijayanagara (modern Hampi, Karnataka)

Founders

Harihara I and Bukka Raya I of the Sangama dynasty

Notable Rulers

Deva Raya II, Krishnadevaraya, Achyuta Deva Raya, Rama Raya

Religion

Predominantly Hinduism, with strong patronage to Vaishnavism and Shaivism

Administration

The Vijayanagar rulers followed a centralized system with provincial divisions known as *Rajyas*. They maintained a powerful military, an efficient tax system, and a strong bureaucracy. The empire was also known for its diplomacy and trade relations with foreign powers.

Achievements

  • Promoted art, architecture, and literature in Kannada, Telugu, Tamil, and Sanskrit.
  • Constructed magnificent temples such as Virupaksha and Vittala temples at Hampi.
  • Encouraged trade with Persia, Portugal, and Southeast Asia.
  • Krishnadevaraya’s reign is remembered as a golden age of South Indian culture.
