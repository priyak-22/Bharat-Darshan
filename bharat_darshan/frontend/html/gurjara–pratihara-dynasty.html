Gurjara–Pratihara Dynasty (c. 8th–11th century CE)

Region: Northern India, centered around Kannauj

Capital

Kannauj (modern Uttar Pradesh)

Notable Rulers

Nagabhata I, Mihira Bhoja (Bhoja I), Mahendrapala I

Religion

Hinduism (mainly Vaishnavism and Shaivism)

Administration

The empire had a feudal system with regional governors. The Pratiharas maintained strong military control and resisted Arab invasions in Western India.

Achievements

  • Successfully defended India from Arab invasions.
  • Kannauj became a major cultural and political center.
  • Encouraged art and architecture; supported temple building.
  • Maintained balance of power among Palas and Rashtrakutas.
