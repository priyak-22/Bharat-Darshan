Muhammad Ghori (Ghurid Dynasty) 1173 – 1206 CE

Region: Northern and Central India

Capital

Pataliputra (modern Patna) and Ujjain (secondary capital)

Notable Rulers

  • Chandragupta I
  • Samudragupta
  • Chandragupta II (Vikramaditya)
  • Kumaragupta I
  • Skandagupta

Religion

Predominantly Hinduism, with tolerance toward Buddhism and Jainism

Administration

The Gupta Empire followed a monarchical system with a well-organized bureaucracy. Provinces (Bhuktis) were governed by Uparikas, and districts (Vishayas) were managed by Vishayapatis. Local administration was efficient and supported by guilds.

Achievements

  • Known as the "Golden Age of India" for advancements in art, science, and literature
  • Flourishing of Sanskrit literature (Kalidasa, Aryabhata)
  • Development of classical Indian sculpture and architecture
  • Expansion of trade and prosperity across the subcontinent
  • Introduction of the decimal system and zero in mathematics
