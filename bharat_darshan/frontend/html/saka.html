Saka Dynasty (c. 2nd century BCE – 4th century CE)

Region: Western and Northwestern India (Gujarat, Rajasthan, Malwa)

Capital

Ujjain and Taxila

Notable Rulers

  • Maues (Moga)
  • Azes I & II
  • Nahapana
  • Chashtana
  • Rudradaman I

Religion

Hinduism, Buddhism, and Zoroastrian influences

Administration

The Sakas established regional kingdoms ruled by satraps (governors). They adopted Indian titles and administration while maintaining Central Asian traditions.

Achievements

  • Promoted trade between India, Central Asia, and Rome
  • Introduced silver coins with Greco-Indian symbols
  • Encouraged the use of Sanskrit inscriptions (e.g., Junagadh Inscription of Rudradaman I)
  • Patronized art and culture in western India
