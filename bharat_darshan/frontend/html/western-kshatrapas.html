Western Kshatrapas (c. 35–405 CE)

Region: Western India (Gujarat, Maharashtra, Malwa)

Capital

Ujjain and later Junagadh

Notable Rulers

  • Chastana
  • Rudradaman I
  • Rudrasena I
  • Rudrasimha III

Religion

Hinduism, Buddhism, and Jainism were all practiced and supported under their rule.

Administration

The Western Kshatrapas ruled as satraps (governors) similar to their Indo-Scythian predecessors. They issued numerous inscriptions and silver coins bearing Greek and Brahmi scripts.

Achievements

  • Issued well-designed silver coins that influenced later Indian coinage
  • Rudradaman I composed the Junagadh inscription in Sanskrit, one of the earliest known examples
  • Promoted irrigation works and trade across western India
