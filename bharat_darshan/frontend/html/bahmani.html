Bahmani Sultanate (1347–1527 CE)

Region: Deccan Plateau, centered around Gulbarga and Bidar

Capital

Gulbarga (later Bidar, both in present-day Karnataka)

Notable Rulers

Ala-ud-Din Hasan Bahman Shah, Firoz Shah Bahmani, Ahmad Shah I Wali

Religion

Islam (Sunni, with Persian cultural influence)

Administration

The Bahmani Sultanate had a centralized administration influenced by Persian models. Provinces called *Tarafs* were governed by nobles known as *Tarafdars*. The court promoted Persian art, language, and architecture.

Achievements

  • Established the first independent Muslim kingdom in the Deccan.
  • Encouraged Persian art, architecture, and culture in South India.
  • Built impressive structures like the Jama Masjid and Bidar Fort.
  • Laid the foundation for later Deccan Sultanates such as Bijapur and Golconda.
