Talikota

Region: Bengal and Bihar | Capital: Pataliputra, later Vikramashila and Nalanda

Capital

Pataliputra, Vikramashila, and Nalanda

Notable Rulers

  • Gopala (Founder)
  • Dharmapala
  • Devapala
  • Mahipala I

Religion

Buddhism (Mahayana and Vajrayana); also supported Hinduism.

Administration

The Pala Empire had a centralized administration led by kings with a strong military and regional governors. They emphasized education and diplomacy, maintaining ties with Southeast Asia and Tibet.

Achievements

  • Restored political unity in eastern India after a period of anarchy
  • Established major Buddhist universities—Nalanda, Vikramashila, and Odantapuri
  • Encouraged trade and art across Bengal and Bihar
  • Spread Indian culture and Buddhism to Southeast Asia and Tibet
