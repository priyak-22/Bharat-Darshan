Maukharis Dynasty (6th century CE)

Region: Northern India | Capital: Kannauj

Capital

Kannauj (in present-day Uttar Pradesh)

Notable Rulers

  • Harivarman (Founder)
  • Isanavarman
  • Sharvavarman
  • Grahavarman

Religion

Primarily Hinduism, with notable patronage of Shaivism and Vaishnavism.

Administration

The Maukharis ruled from Kannauj with a feudal administrative system. They were known for military strength and for resisting the Gupta decline and Huna invasions. The kingdom later allied with Harshavardhana’s Vardhana dynasty through marriage ties.

Achievements

  • Reasserted Indian control over the Ganges plain after Huna invasions
  • Strengthened Kannauj as a political and cultural center
  • Maintained strong ties with neighboring dynasties, paving the way for Harsha’s empire
